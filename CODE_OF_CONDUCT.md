# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We are committed to making participation in Cogniform welcoming and free from
harassment for everyone, regardless of background, identity, experience, or
level of contribution.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, specific, and constructive.
- Assume good intent while addressing harmful impact directly.
- Keep technical disagreement focused on evidence and project outcomes.
- Respect privacy and do not publish another person's private information.
- Accept moderator decisions and make space for less-heard contributors.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized attention, deliberate
intimidation, sustained disruption, and abusive or demeaning language are not
acceptable in project spaces or when representing the project.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions, comments, issues, or other
participation that violates this code. Proportionate responses may include a
private correction, warning, temporary restriction, or permanent ban.

Report conduct concerns privately using contact information published on the
repository owner's GitHub profile. If no private channel is available, open a
minimal issue asking a maintainer to establish private contact; do not include
sensitive details in that issue. Security vulnerabilities follow
[SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md), not the conduct-reporting path.

Maintainers will protect reporters' privacy as far as reasonably possible and
will disclose conflicts of interest when handing a report to another moderator.

## Scope and attribution

This code applies in all Cogniform community spaces and when an individual is
officially representing the project. It is adapted from the
[Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct.html).
